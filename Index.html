Welcome to Godman Apostolic Ministries

Located in the heart of ile-ife Osun state Nigeria, Godman Apostolic Ministries is a vibrant and dynamic church community that was birthed on February 1st, 2025, by our esteemed man of God, Pastor Joseph Martins. With a passion to raise spiritual armies, our church is built on the core values of unveiling the Father and revealing the Son. At Godman Apostolic Ministries, we are committed to creating an atmosphere where believers can grow, thrive, and fulfill their divine purpose.

Our Core Values

Church is guided by a set of core values that shape our beliefs, actions, and interactions. These values include: Honor, Love, prayer, Faith, Word, Identity and many more..

Meet the Choir Department

As a department birthed on March 1st, 2025, the choir is an integral part of our ministry, dedicated to bringing the word of God to people through song and melody. We are a people of love, worshipping in the spirit and guided by the principles of our church. Our musicians are vessels of God's sound, drawing inspiration from the realm of God to bless and uplift the church.
The choir's mission is to create an atmosphere where people can connect with God on a deeper level through music and worship. We believe in the power of music to transcend boundaries and touch hearts, and we're committed to using this gift to spread the love of Christ.
At Godman Apostolic Ministries, we're passionate about creating a community that is rooted in God's word and driven by His love. Join us as we worship, learn, and grow together in faith! y
